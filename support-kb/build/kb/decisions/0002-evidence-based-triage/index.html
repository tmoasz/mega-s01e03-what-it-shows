Shared parser: month-first interpretation of day-first dates

The shared parser reads month first even when a tenant writes day first. For example, R-41200 reports 7 April becoming 4 July. The supplied classifier assigns day-first app/email reports, and day-first booking-v2 form reports, to this group. Fix parsing against the tenant’s date convention; regression coverage must include ambiguous dates such as 07/04 and a non-ambiguous date such as 23/04. This repo diagnoses the problem; it does not contain or claim to fix the production parser.

Booking-v3: a misleading MM/DD/YYYY hint

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY while reading the entered date in a day-first tenant’s convention. That is a separate user-interface contract problem. For a Spanish booking-v3 tenant, a person following the hint for 6 January may enter 01/06 and get 1 June. R-41230 supplies that symptom. Align the hint with the form’s actual locale interpretation. Test booking-v3 tenants independently of the shared-parser fix.
